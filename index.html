How to be nice

🤝

Leave no letter out

Drag across neighboring letters to make words. A board is done when every letter has been in at least one.

😊

Smiles stay useful

A happy letter can keep helping. Reuse it to reach the shy ones still waiting.

🌅

No timer, no score

Take all the time you like. When the last letter joins in, the family rearranges into a thank-you note of its own.

🦦

Meet the families

Every board is a numbered family that keeps its number for good, so you can tell a friend “try Family 42.”
